About

"Numbers tell a story — I make sure it's an accurate one."

I'm Renu Varshitha, a treasurer and financial analyst based in Galway, Ireland. I'm currently completing my MSc in Corporate Finance at the University of Galway, building on a First Class Honours Bachelor of Commerce from Osmania University, Hyderabad. Alongside my studies, I'm working through the ACCA qualification, currently at 4 of 13 papers.

I'm drawn to the analytical side of banking and finance: portfolio management, variance analysis, and the kind of structured reporting that turns raw transactions into a clear financial story.
